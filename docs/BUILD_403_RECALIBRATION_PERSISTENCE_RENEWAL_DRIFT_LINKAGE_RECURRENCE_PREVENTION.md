# AI Data Platform Build 403 — Recalibration Persistence Validation, Renewal Timing Drift Recovery & Seven-Application Linkage Recurrence Prevention Governance

Build 403 follows Build 402 by proving that forecast recalibration remains effective over time, renewal timing drift is actually recovered, and the seven-application topology does not relapse into stale, mismatched or authority-changing linkage.

## Recalibration persistence validation

Every governed shell requires a 72-hour-or-longer observation window with at least seven samples. The maximum and latest absolute forecast error must both remain at 500 basis points or less. Any recurrence, missing owner review or missing independent verification fails the persistence gate.

## Renewal timing drift recovery

Build 402 established the governed predictive-renewal window. Build 403 verifies recovery when timing moves outside that window. A recovered timing record must start from a drifted lead time, return to the 6–24-hour window, remain observed for at least 24 hours with at least three samples, avoid the projected threshold breach, show no recurrence, and be owner-reviewed plus independently verified.

If no timing drift occurred, the record must still prove that no renewal was required or that the previously optimal lead time remained unchanged.

## Seven-application linkage recurrence prevention

All seven applications require current recurrence-prevention evidence:
- AI Data Platform
- Rosevear Comms Hub
- Rosevear AI Hub
- Yard Workers
- iCamp
- Devil n Dove
- Rosie Dazzlers

Each record must retain the canonical repository and topology 'rosevear-master-shell-seven-apps.v1', current production-main and manifest hashes, correct self identity, canonical topology parity and AI Data Platform validation authority. The prevention window must span at least 72 hours with at least three samples per application.

Linkage recurrence, stale-evidence recurrence or any detected authority transfer blocks closure. Evidence must remain no more than 24 hours old, and authority transfer remains forbidden.

Production GREEN cannot substitute for persistence, recovery or recurrence-prevention evidence.
