# AI Data Platform Build 400 — Confidence Early-Warning Response Effectiveness, Long-Horizon Assurance Degradation Recovery & Cross-Shell Predictive Renewal Governance

Build 400 turns Build 399 early warning into measured response effectiveness, sustained assurance-degradation recovery and predictive renewal decisions.

## Confidence early-warning response effectiveness

Every governed shell records whether a warning required action. A triggered response requires at least a 24-hour observation window, at least three samples, at least 250 basis points of confidence recovery from the projected low point, restoration to at least 7000 basis points, owner review and independent verification. No-warning shells record an explicit NOT_REQUIRED outcome.

## Long-horizon assurance degradation recovery

Every shell records whether assurance degradation occurred. A degradation recovery requires at least 72 hours of evidence, at least as many recovered renewals as degraded renewals, no missed renewals, current evidence, no recurrence, owner review and independent verification.

## Cross-shell predictive renewal governance

Every shell receives a 72-hour-or-longer confidence forecast based on at least five samples and five evidence points. Forecasted confidence drops above 500 basis points, or projected confidence below 7000 basis points, require an advance renewal schedule. Stable forecasts remain CURRENT.

Deployment GREEN cannot substitute for warning-response, degradation-recovery or predictive-renewal evidence.
