## Standing safety rules

Future work must continue to preserve:

- no raw-secret exposure;
- no automatic mutating capability activation;
- authoritative workspace/tenant isolation;
- idempotency and replay protection;
- append-only evidence for consequential workflows;
- explicit owner approval for event replay, credentials, connection activation, trust changes and peer writes;
- QL-266 remains held unless independently satisfied by authentic evidence.
