# AI Data Platform — Owner-Gated Roadmap Builds 415–424

This roadmap begins only after Build 414 software release. Each build remains independently owner-authorized and protocol 1.1 compatible. No future build is automatically executed by roadmap approval.

1. **Build 415 — Live Evidence Freshness, Expiry SLA & Cross-Application Renewal Control.** Add bounded freshness SLAs, renewal queues, expiry alerts and evidence-aging dashboards across all seven applications without converting stale evidence into success. **Implemented in Build 415:** 49 live freshness slots, bounded domain SLAs, fail-closed aging states, append-only expiry alerts and governed renewal requests that never manufacture fresh evidence.
2. **Build 416 — Original-Host Service Continuity, Reboot Witness & Independent Runtime Custody.** Strengthen signed reboot/startup receipts, service lifecycle evidence and original-host continuity for the three Server Shell workers. **Implemented in Build 416:** current-source contract review pinned to exact Hub/Data/Comms revisions, fixed Windows SCM identity and Automatic startup policy targets, protected boot-witness/peer-ACK/rotation ledgers, explicit NOT IMPLEMENTED sink/installer gaps, 24-hour continuity and rollback requirements; no physical activation.
3. **Build 417 — Communications Delivery SLA, ACK Latency, Replay Quality & Dead-Letter Operations.** Measure Comms Hub delivery, acknowledgement latency, replay outcomes, duplicate suppression and dead-letter recovery across approved relationships. **Implemented in Build 417:** append-only protected delivery/ACK and dead-letter recovery ledgers, 14-relationship fail-closed SLA assessment, 120-second ACK target, 300-second queue-to-delivery target, 24-hour recovery window, maximum three replay attempts, duplicate suppression evidence, shared 12-theme Master catalogue and responsive form/date-picker cosmetic refinement.
4. **Build 418 — Cross-Application Recovery Exercise & Rollback Readiness Certification.** Run evidence-backed supervised recovery and rollback exercises while preserving local authority and explicit owner gates. **Implemented in Build 418:** 28 fail-closed recovery exercise slots across seven applications and four recovery domains, seven source-owned rollback-readiness receipts, independently witnessed cross-application exercise evidence, owner/witness separation, post-recovery health/integrity checks, known-good revision/restore-point/migration compatibility evidence and explicit prohibition on automatic recovery or rollback.
5. **Build 419 — Shared Performance Budgets, Capacity Forecasting & Vercel Usage Guardrails.** Coordinate performance and capacity evidence while allowing each application to release independently and reducing unnecessary deployment usage. **Implemented in Build 419:** 35 source-owned performance-budget slots across seven applications and five domains, seven capacity-forecast receipts with honest unknown-headroom handling, seven Vercel-hosting dispositions, owner-defined 24-hour deployment budgets for hosted apps, duplicate-deployment/exact-SHA reuse tracking, preview-migration safety and explicit no-invented-plan-limit/no-invented-billing governance.
6. **Build 420 — Accessibility, Device & Degraded-Network Re-Certification.** Re-certify desktop/tablet/mobile, keyboard, contrast, reduced-motion, loading, offline/degraded and recovery behaviour across all applications. **Implemented in Build 420:** 63 fresh evidence slots across seven applications and nine dimensions; desktop/tablet/mobile viewport acceptance, 44 px mobile touch-target floor, keyboard-only/focus/screen-reader checks, forced-colors and AA contrast, reduced-motion behavior, loading/empty/error/retry states, offline/degraded-network handling and data-preserving duplicate-safe recovery. Synthetic, stale, PARTIAL or deployment-only evidence cannot re-certify.
7. **Build 421 — Tenant Isolation, Consent Expiry & Delegated-Capability Renewal.** Renew least-privilege, tenant/workspace boundaries, consent state, expiry and revocation evidence across product and internal applications. **Implemented in Build 421:** 21 application-local renewal slots across seven applications for least privilege, tenant/workspace isolation and consent lifecycle; 28 directional delegated-capability renewal receipts; fail-closed expiry/revocation handling; exact capability/audience scope; maximum 24-hour delegated lifetime; no self-renewal, subdelegation, authority transfer or T5 delegation; and an evidence-backed 5–10 percentage-point feature-completion impact rule for future queued builds.
8. **Build 422 — Data Lifecycle, Retention, Deletion & DLP Renewal Assurance.** Re-certify classification, encryption, redaction, retention, deletion evidence and export controls without exposing secret values.
9. **Build 423 — Operator Acceptance, Role Separation & Production Workflow Ceremony.** Collect fresh owner/admin/worker/customer acceptance where applicable and verify that consequential workflows retain role and authority separation.
10. **Build 424 — Operational Maturity Closure, Security Renewal & Next Roadmap Ceremony.** Close only evidence-proven targets, renew security and communications assurance and publish the next owner-gated roadmap.

## Standing rules

- Missing, UNKNOWN, STALE, HOLD or failed evidence never becomes progress.
- Each future queued build must consolidate enough meaningful feature-list scope to support an evidence-backed 5–10 percentage-point completion movement; otherwise adjacent scope should be combined before promotion.
- Production GREEN never implies a peer application, Windows service, Home Assistant, MQTT, camera or physical device actually performed an action.
- Rosevear Comms Hub retains communications authority.
- AI Data Platform retains validation/evidence authority.
- Rosevear AI Hub retains local physical-automation authority.
- Every source application retains its own operational authority.
- Ordinary additive changes remain independently releasable under protocol 1.1.
