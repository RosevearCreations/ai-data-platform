# AI Data Platform Build 404 — Long-Horizon Recalibration Persistence Stability, Renewal Drift Recovery Effectiveness & Seven-Application Linkage Prevention Assurance Governance

Build 404 follows Build 403 by extending recurrence prevention into long-horizon assurance. It verifies that forecast recalibration stays stable for a full seven-day evidence window, that any renewal timing drift recovery remains effective, and that all seven application links remain current and recurrence-free.

## Long-horizon recalibration stability

Every governed shell requires a seven-day-or-longer observation window with at least 14 samples. Maximum and latest forecast error must remain at 500 basis points or less, with zero samples outside the governed error budget and zero recurrence events. Owner review and independent verification remain mandatory.

## Renewal drift recovery effectiveness

Recovery effectiveness is observed for at least a 72-hour window with at least five samples. When timing drift occurred, every detected drift event must be recovered, the latest post-recovery lead time must remain inside the governed 6–24-hour window, and there must be no threshold breaches or recurrence.

When no timing drift occurred, the record explicitly remains NOT_REQUIRED while still proving a clean observation window.

## Seven-application linkage prevention assurance

All seven applications require current assurance evidence:
- AI Data Platform
- Rosevear AI Hub
- Rosevear Comms Hub
- iCamp
- Yard Workers
- Devil n Dove
- Rosie Dazzlers

Each application must retain its canonical repository, topology 'rosevear-master-shell-seven-apps.v1', a valid production-main SHA and manifest hash, current evidence no more than 24 hours old, a seven-day-or-longer observation window, at least seven samples, correct self identity, canonical topology parity and AI Data Platform validation authority.

Any continuity break, stale-evidence recurrence or authority transfer blocks assurance. Authority transfer remains forbidden.

Production GREEN cannot substitute for long-horizon recalibration, recovery-effectiveness or linkage-assurance evidence.
