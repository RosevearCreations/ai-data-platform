# AI Data Platform — Builds 351–360 Control Maturity Roadmap

This roadmap follows the Build 350 renewal checkpoint. The sequence finishes basic controls, progresses through moderate controls and then opens the first advanced-control layer.

Only work explicitly authorized by the owner may be implemented or promoted. This document does not make deployment GREEN equivalent to source acceptance, owner acceptance or Master Completion.

## Basic controls

1. **Build 351 — Master Basic Controls Completion, Coverage Gaps & Operational Truth Reconciliation** — current authorized build; reconcile foundational controls, separate implementation from live acceptance, remove obsolete preflight/prelaunch blockers and retain current evidence-backed requirements.
2. **Build 352 — Basic Operator Actions, Safe Refresh, Retry, Recheck & Recovery Controls** — queued roadmap candidate.
3. **Build 353 — Basic Alerts, Thresholds, Ownership, Escalation & Resolution Tracking** — queued roadmap candidate.

## Moderate controls

4. **Build 354 — Moderate Cross-Application Control Center & Dependency-Aware Operations** — queued roadmap candidate.
5. **Build 355 — Moderate Performance Optimization, Caching, Query & Rendering Control Plane** — queued roadmap candidate.
6. **Build 356 — Moderate Capacity, Workload, Resource & Demand Forecasting** — queued roadmap candidate.
7. **Build 357 — Moderate Incident Automation, Runbooks & Supervised Recovery Orchestration** — queued roadmap candidate.

## Advanced controls

8. **Build 358 — Advanced Anomaly Detection, Behaviour Baselines & Predictive Risk Intelligence** — queued roadmap candidate.
9. **Build 359 — Advanced Adaptive Controls, Dynamic Thresholds & Intelligent Resource Management** — queued roadmap candidate.
10. **Build 360 — Advanced Control Readiness, Supervised Self-Healing & Autonomous-Control Boundary Certification** — queued roadmap candidate.

## Control-maturity rules

- measure first, optimize second, automate third;
- missing, expired or stale evidence is not success;
- implementation coverage and live acceptance remain separate;
- historical preflight/prelaunch labels are informational unless a current requirement explicitly references them;
- source-specific trust, consent, owner acceptance and rollback gates remain authoritative;
- consequential peer writes require explicit authorization;
- advanced controls must retain bounded scope, rollback, audit evidence and operator override.
